A bit about me.

I'm Tom Ostapchuk, a product leader, documentary filmmaker, and father of two based in Guelph, Ontario. I have more than 10 years of experience across product management, marketing, and technology, including work at EV Connect and science communications with Natural Resources Canada. I founded Yellow Boat Media to bring together my interests in conservation, tourism, and storytelling. Outside work, I enjoy cycling, running, and exploring Canada's outdoors. I'm drawn to work that combines creativity, independence, and a positive environmental impact.

My business ↗ Yellow Boat Media I founded Yellow Boat Media, a Guelph-based video production business creating documentary-style films for tourism, conservation, and government organizations. The work spans science communications, brand stories, and short social videos that connect audiences with people, places, and environmental initiatives.
